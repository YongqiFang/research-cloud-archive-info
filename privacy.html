Research Cloud Archive

Privacy notice

Last updated: October 1, 2026.

Research Cloud Archive is a personal research archive workflow using a local DVC client and Google Drive. This notice describes that workflow and this informational website.

Files, metadata, and Google permissions

The local client processes the archive files selected by the user, associated file and remote metadata, and the credentials needed to authenticate. It uploads or downloads files between the user's computer and Google Drive as directed by the user.

Google OAuth authorization requests the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive and https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata scopes. The Drive permission allows broad access to Drive files, including reading and managing files; the appdata permission allows access to application-specific data. The application therefore can access Google Drive data, even though this static website does not.

Storage and sharing

The local client saves its credentials and configuration locally on the user's computer. Archive files and metadata are processed locally and stored in the user's Google Drive as needed for the workflow. Files are transferred to and from Google Drive; their access and sharing are governed by the user's Drive settings. This website does not receive those files, metadata, or credentials.

Retention and user controls

The user manages retention and deletion of archive data in Google Drive and on their computer. Revoking OAuth authorization does not itself delete existing archive files. Local credentials and configuration remain until the user removes them through the client's configuration or local file management.

The user can review and revoke the application's access in their Google Account third-party connections. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

This static website and its host

These pages do not process OAuth, store tokens, collect archive files, use analytics scripts or tracking cookies, or offer forms. They use only local styling and do not load external fonts or other embedded resources.

GitHub Pages hosts this website. GitHub may collect technical information about visits, including IP addresses and access logs, under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. The absence of website analytics does not mean the hosting platform collects no information.

Questions and configuration feedback

Feedback can be submitted through this repository's public Issues. Issues are public: do not post private files, research details, credentials, tokens, or OAuth configuration files. No response time is promised.
